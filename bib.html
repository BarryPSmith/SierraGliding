Season: March - September
Approach: 1 hour from the highway; AWD required; 40 minute hike
Parking Coordinates: 37.3814, -118.6325
Launch Coordinates: 37.3765, -118.6242
LZ Coordinates: None
Launch Time: 11am - 1pm

Description: With no easy LZ, this is an XC site where you do not want to bomb out. Fortunately, it is very consistent when in season. The Bib, and its surrounding Round Valley, typically experiences less wind than other launches in our area; it is often launchable when other sites are not. This site remains flyable even with moderate ridge-top westerly wind. Early and late season XC potential can be limited to the end of the Wheeler Crest to the north and Coyote Flat to the south. Logistics can be a pain, given the lack of good landing locations close to where one parks the car.

Approach: Leave Hwy 395 at Pine Creek Road, and drive to the hamlet of Rovanna. Turn left onto Virginia St then left again at the end then right onto a dirt road, driving around the toe of the moraine. Follow the dirt road to the Elderberry Canyon trailhead.

Park here, and cross the talus field to the south, picking up the old overgrown road on the far side. Hike up this road for five switchbacks, then leave the gully on the east side and gain the bench on the ridge. Cross the bench to east end, where you will find the weather station and old shadecloth at launch.
