Approach: 15 minutes from highway, 2wd dirt
Launch Coordinates: 37.4199, -118.4289

Description: A ridge soaring site, over the ~250'' high edge of the Volcanic Tabelands. Can be tricky to get the right conditions: the wind-strength window between bombing out and being blown backwards on launch is small. Or maybe I should just learn to launch in high winds.

Obvious warning: Flying here during thermic conditions can be dangerous.

Approach: Leave the 395 onto Hwy 6 in Bishop. After 1.6 miles, turn left onto Five Bridge Road. Pass through the concrete plant and continue straight at the five-way intersection. Take the third left (3.9 miles from Hwy 6) after you gain the plateau, and continue to the end of that road.
