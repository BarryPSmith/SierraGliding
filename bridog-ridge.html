Approach: long, awd required.
Launch Coordinates: 37.7052, -118.6709

Description: The difficult logistics of this site make it unpopular, but it offers good XC potential on the right day. Pleasant landing options below launch are scarce; if you have to you can put it down in the sagebrush or on a narrow road, and be careful of the power lines.

Approach: Note: Google Map directions to the launch coordinates will lead you down closed forest service roads.
Leave Hwy 395 at the green church onto Benton Crossing Road. After 10.5 miles, turn left onto Watterson Troughs Road. Follow this for 5.5 miles, then turn left onto 3S01B (37.6762, -118.6379). Turn left again in 0.8 miles (37.6861, -118.6332). After 1.8 miles, merge north onto a road running along the ridge, and follow this for 0.6 miles before parking on top of a hill. Walk five minutes down the close road to the west to arrive at launch.
