Season: May - October
Approach: 40 minutes from the highway, AWD required
Launch Coordinates: 37.5892, -118.8175
LZ Coordinates: 37.6007, -118.7985

Description: A very pleasant launch over Long Valley, our most popular option once the road melts out. McGee is the closest launch to Bishop and Mammoth that doesn''t require hiking or a real 4wd. All types of flying, except ridge soaring, are common here.

Don''t land in the triangle of land next to the 395 containing the two red barns, continue flying over the fence and land in the large green arc. Beware of marshy conditions on the right side of the green arc, aim to land close to the sagebrush.

Approach: Leave the 395 onto Mount Morrison Road, then turn left at the cemetery. Continue on this dirt road and do not make any turns until you arrive at launch. You will pass a turnoff to the left, ignore it. You will pass through a cattle gate, open it (and close it again!) if necessary. You will pass turnoffs to the right, and a 4 way intersection, keep straight always. You will go up 8 switchbacks on the NW slope of the mountain, before the much longer 9th switchback takes you onto the NE slope. Park at the far end of that switchback, launch is 30 yards back down the road, below the road.
