Season: All year, but summer can be difficult
Approach: 30-70 minutes from highway, depending on vehicle capabilities
Parking Coordinates: AWD 37.3952, -118.2950; 4x4 on launch; 2wd at the LZ. Launch Coordiantes: 37.3879, -118.2954 LZ Coordinates: 37.3885, -118.3165

Description: Flynns is the catch-all site on the Whites, featuring easy logistics and year round accessibility. Winter sled rides and soaring sessions, shoulder season XC, and summer evening flights are all popular here. The house thermal is above launch, and getting into it can be tricky; it is sometimes known as "Dirty Rotten Flynns" by pilots looking at a beautiful day and wondering why they are in the LZ fifteen minutes after launch, contemplating the gruelling hike back up the face in the middle of a summer day.

Approach: To the LZ:
From Bishop and south: Leave the 395 at the main intersection in Bishop, heading towards the Whites on East Line Street. Continue down this for 3.5 miles, then turn left onto Laws Poleta Road. In 2 miles, turn right at a cattle guard onto a dirt road and remain south of the canal. Follow this to where it crosses the canal on the east end of the fields, this is the shade tree and LZ.
From the north: Leave the 395 in Bishop to head north on Hwy 6. After 3.7 miles, turn right onto Silver Canyon Road and drive past the Laws Railroad Museum. In one mile, turn right onto Laws Poleta Road, and follow that for 1 mile before turning left at a cattle guard onto a dirt road, after two canal crossings. Remain south of the canal, and follow the dirt road to where it crosses the canal on the east end of the fields, this is the shade tree and LZ.

To Launch:
2wd: Park at the shade tree and hike up the obvious trail to launch. About 1 hour hiking.
awd: Drive up the canyon north of launch until at an obvious pullout on the right with a foot trail heading up a side canyon. Hike up that trail to launch, about 20 minutes hiking.
4wd: Drive up the canyon north of launch, and follow the road around all the way to launch.
